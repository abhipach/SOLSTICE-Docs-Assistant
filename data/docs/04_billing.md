# Billing and Invoices

## Billing cycles
Plans are billed either monthly or annually. Monthly invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Annual invoices are issued on the anniversary of your subscription start date. Invoices are payable within 30 days (Net 30).

## Changing your billing cycle
Only an Owner can change the cycle, from Billing, then Plan. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately and unused monthly time is credited. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect at your next renewal.

## Adding seats
Seats added in the middle of a cycle are prorated and appear on your next invoice. Seats freed by deactivated users are removed at the next billing cycle.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express, and ACH. Annual Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer.

## Invoices and receipts
Download invoices as PDF from Billing, then Invoices. Add extra billing contacts and a VAT ID under Billing details.

## Refunds
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the initial purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months.
