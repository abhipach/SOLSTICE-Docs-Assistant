# Reports and Data Exports

## Available reports
Solstice includes three standard reports: Course Completion, Learner Activity and Assessment Results. Report data refreshes every 4 hours, so very recent activity may not appear immediately.

## Export limits
Exports are available as CSV or XLSX. The Standard plan allows up to 10,000 rows per export. The Enterprise plan allows up to 100,000 rows per export. Each admin can run at most 20 exports per day. Download links expire after 24 hours.

## Exporting large datasets
If your data exceeds the row limit, narrow the export with a date range or a group filter and export in several parts. Enterprise customers can also schedule a weekly export that is emailed to a chosen recipient.
