# Courses and Content

## Uploading content
Solstice accepts SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 and xAPI packages up to 2 GB each. Video files must be MP4 and can be up to 5 GB.

## Assigning courses
Assign a course to individual users or to groups and optionally set a due date. Automatic reminders are sent 7 days and 1 day before the due date.

## Completion criteria
The default passing score for assessments is 80 percent. Admins can change the passing score per course. A learner who passes receives a PDF certificate automatically.
